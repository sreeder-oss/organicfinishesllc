Moroccan Tadelakt is a traditional hydraulic lime plaster compressed with river stones and treated with olive oil soap for an old-world, sculptural stone look on shower walls and wet rooms. Microcement is a polymer-modified, mesh-reinforced mineral overlay sealed with multi-coat 2-component polyurethane (including anti-slip floor additives), making it tough enough for curbless shower floors, bathroom vanities, countertops, whole-home floors, and high-traffic commercial storefronts.
